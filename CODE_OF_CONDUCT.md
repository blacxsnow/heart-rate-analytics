# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a respectful, evidence-oriented place for people
with different backgrounds, devices, and levels of experience. We pledge to
make participation harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique code, evidence, and methods, not people.
- Assume good faith while asking for reproducible evidence.
- Respect privacy: never post MAC addresses, device IDs, account IDs, keys, or
  personal health data.
- Follow the hardware-safety rules in `CONTRIBUTING.md` and `SECURITY.md`.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private data, captures, identifiers, or health
  records.
- Encouraging destructive firmware modification, signature bypass, credential
  extraction, or unsafe hardware operations.
- Medical diagnosis or clinical advice presented as a project feature.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments that violate
this code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report concerns privately through the repository's security/advisory channel or
to the maintainers via GitHub. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical.
